## 👨‍💻 About Me

I am a game developer with over **10 years of software development experience** and **8+ years focused specifically on game development**.

Throughout my career, I have developed and contributed to **60+ game projects**, ranging from prototypes to full-scale commercial releases on **Google Play** and the **App Store**.

I specialize in:

- 🎯 Core Gameplay Systems  
- 🏗 Clean & Modular Architecture  
- ⚡ Optimization & Performance  
- 💰 Scalable Economy Systems  
- 🧠 Server-Authoritative Roblox Systems  

Currently based in **Antalya, Turkey 🇹🇷**, I am the **Co-Founder of COLE Games**, an independent studio focused on building scalable, engaging and long-term sustainable game experiences.
